Privacy Policy

A simple privacy note for Alta Edit readers.

Alta Edit keeps data collection minimal and uses reader information only for the purpose it was provided.

Information you provide

If you contact Alta Edit or join a future newsletter, you may provide information such as your name, email address, Instagram handle or message content. Alta Edit uses that information to respond or send requested updates.

Affiliate links and third parties

Alta Edit may link to retailers, affiliate networks, Instagram and other third-party websites. Those sites may collect information under their own privacy policies.

Analytics and site operation

Alta Edit may use basic analytics or hosting logs to understand site performance and reader interest. This information is used in aggregate where possible.

Updates

This policy may be updated as Alta Edit adds newsletter, analytics or affiliate tools. Please check this page for the latest version.
