Founder note

A practical edit for a persistent gap.

I started Alta Edit because finding beautiful shoes in larger women’s sizes still takes too much work. The best styles often stop just before the sizes many tall women actually need.

Alta Edit is my place to collect the finds, brands and fit shortcuts that make the search easier.
